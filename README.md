# Mobile Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The Mobile Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze mobile sales performance and provide insights into sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, and payment preferences. The project includes dedicated dashboard pages for overall sales analysis, Month-to-Date (MTD) performance, and Same Period Last Year comparisons.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall mobile sales performance.
- Analyze sales across different cities.
- Identify top-performing mobile brands and models.
- Track monthly sales trends and transaction volumes.
- Understand customer ratings and preferred payment methods.
- Monitor current month-to-date sales performance.
- Compare current sales performance with the equivalent period in the previous year.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive reporting.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard design and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — KPI calculations, time intelligence, and sales measures

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Transactions
- Average Sales Value
- Month-to-Date (MTD) Sales
- Previous Year's Sales for the Equivalent Period
- Sales Growth Percentage, where applicable

## Dashboard Pages & Features

### 1. Mobile Sales Overview
Provides a consolidated view of mobile sales performance through KPI cards and interactive visualizations.

- **City-wise Analysis:** Compare sales performance across cities.
- **Monthly Trends:** Track changes in sales over time.
- **Brand & Model Analysis:** Compare the performance of mobile brands and models.
- **Customer Ratings:** Analyze customer feedback.
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Understand customer payment preferences.
- **Interactive Filters:** Explore sales data using dashboard slicers.

### 2. Month-to-Date (MTD) Dashboard
Focuses on sales performance from the beginning of a selected month up to the selected date.

- Monitor current month-to-date sales and related performance metrics.
- Track sales progress throughout the month.
- Analyze sales trends within the selected month.
- Support timely monitoring of business performance and monthly targets, where applicable.

### 3. Same Period Last Year Dashboard
Enables year-over-year sales analysis by comparing the selected period with the equivalent period in the previous year.

- Compare current-period sales with the corresponding period last year.
- Identify increases or decreases in sales performance.
- Evaluate year-over-year sales trends.
- Support historical performance analysis and business planning.

## Key DAX & Time Intelligence Concepts

- **TOTALMTD:** Calculates month-to-date values within the selected date context.
- **SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR:** Returns the equivalent date period from the previous year.
- **CALCULATE:** Evaluates measures under modified filter contexts.
- **DIVIDE:** Supports percentage calculations, such as sales growth.
